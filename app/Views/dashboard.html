Issue a new asset

Builds the off-chain dossier, hashes it, then your operator wallet (the factory owner) deploys the share token with that hash committed on-chain.

Proof of backing custodian is simulated

The custodian signs how many shares are backed; your wallet relays it on-chain. If it reports fewer backed shares than exist, the marketplace refuses every purchase of that asset until a full attestation is published. Use this to demo the failure case.

Recover a lost or frozen wallet

After re-verifying the same person on a new wallet (it must already be registered on-chain), move all of the old wallet's shares and unclaimed yield to it. Works on frozen wallets; refuses any wallet that is not KYC-eligible. Only the asset owner (operator wallet) can do this, and the reference is recorded on-chain.
